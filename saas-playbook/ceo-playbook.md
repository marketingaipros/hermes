# CEO Playbook

## Purpose

This document is your single source of truth for company focus. If you're confused about what to do, what to prioritize, or why we're doing something — it's here.

## Our Identity

- **What we are:** A bootstrapped SaaS company building products with real customer demand
- **What we are not:** A VC-backed startup chasing hypergrowth, a forever company, a lifestyle business
- **Our competitive advantage:** We move fast because we're small. We make better products because we talk to customers. We exit bigger because we control our destiny.

## The CEO's Only Three Questions

Every week, every meeting, every decision should answer these:

1. **Are we closer to product-market fit?** (If below 1M ARR)
   - More customers talking to us?
   - Higher retention?
   - Stronger word-of-mouth?
   
2. **Are we closer to being replaceable in our own business?**
   - Are systems running without us?
   - Are processes documented?
   - Could we take a 2-week vacation and nothing breaks?

3. **Are we closer to an 8-figure exit?**
   - Are revenue/profit growing?
   - Is the business cleaner and more defensible?
   - Are we building something someone would buy?

If the answer to all three is "yes" every week, we're on track.

## Decision Matrix

When evaluating any decision, score it 1-5 on these dimensions:

| Criterion | Score 1 | Score 5 |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| Customer impact | No customer benefit | Major customer benefit |
| Systemization | Makes us more replaceable | Makes us less replaceable |
| Cost efficiency | Wastes cash | Preserves/grows cash |
| Exit value | Neutral to exit | Increases exit value |
| Speed | Slows us down | Accelerates us |

**Rule:** If a decision scores below 3 on more than 2 criteria, kill it or rework it.

## The "Should We?" Filter

Before making any decision, run it through these yes/no questions:

1. Does a real customer need this? (Not a hypothetical one)
2. Have we validated this with conversations/pre-sales?
3. Can we do this manually before automating?
4. Does this keep headcount under our target?
5. Does this make the business more valuable for exit?
6. Can we afford this without external funding?

**If any answer is "no" — pause. Rethink.**

## What We Never Do

- Hire a salesperson before 1M ARR (co-founder handles sales)
- Hire support before 1M ARR (founders handle support)
- Spend on a CRM (spreadsheets work fine)
- Rent an office
- Build features without paying beta testers
- Run ads before PMF
- Post on social media for "brand building" (post for content-market fit, not vanity)
- Take VC money
- Copy competitors "but cheaper"
- Hire a "VP of X" before 10M ARR
- Automate before we understand the manual process

## What We Always Do

- Talk to at least 10 customers per week
- Write everything down (SOPs, processes, decisions)
- Keep expenses at ramen-level
- Ship fast, iterate faster
- Build in public (LinkedIn, Twitter — founder-led content)
- Track NPS and retention obsessively
- Ask: "Could we sell this right now?" (at any stage)
- Read and reread The E-Myth Revisited
- Ask Adam Robinson's question: "Would customers be livid if we took our product away?"

## Crisis Protocol

When things go sideways (and they will):

1. **Cut spending immediately** — get to zero burn within 72 hours
2. **Talk to every customer** — find out what they still value
3. **Kill what isn't working** — shut down underperforming features, channels, products
4. **Double down on what is** — where's the organic growth coming from?
5. **Revisit PMF** — are we actually product-market fit or just stubborn?

## Weekly CEO Review

Every Friday, answer these:

- What did we ship?
- How many customer conversations did we have?
- What did we learn?
- What did we delete/disable/shut down?
- Are our systems better or worse than last week?
- What's the one thing that, if fixed, would move the needle most?

## Monthly CEO Review

- Review the 3 questions (PMF, replaceability, exit value)
- Check burn rate vs revenue
- Review hiring (should we have hired anyone? were we wrong?)
- Review processes — what needs documentation?
- Where is organic growth coming from? Where is it dying?
