# SaaS Playbook — Master Index

## What Is This?

A living playbook that combines two philosophies:

- **The E-Myth Revisited** by Michael Gerber — build a business that works without you
- **0-to-10M SaaS Playbook** by Adam Robinson — bootstrap, sell before build, exit at 8 figures

Together they answer: *How do we build, grow, and sell a SaaS company?*

## Documents

| Document | What It Is | When to Read |
|----------|-----------|--------------|
| [mission.md](mission.md) | One-page thesis, exit target, non-negotiables | When you need to remember WHY we exist |
| [ceo-playbook.md](ceo-playbook.md) | CEO's single source of truth for focus | When you're confused about priorities |
| [decision-framework.md](decision-framework.md) | Filter for every business decision | Before making any decision |
| [operational-cadence.md](operational-cadence.md) | Weekly/monthly/quarterly rhythms | When you need to know what to do this week |

## How to Use This

1. **Read mission.md first** — this is your north star
2. **Bookmark ceo-playbook.md** — go here when confused
3. **Use decision-framework.md** — every meeting, every call
4. **Follow operational-cadence.md** — the rhythm that makes it stick
