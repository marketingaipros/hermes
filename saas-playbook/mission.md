# Mission & Vision

## The One-Page Thesis

**We build SaaS products that generate real revenue from day one, systematize them so they run without us, and sell them for 8-figure exits.**

Not raise VC. Not hire a big team. Not become employees of our own company.

## Why This Exists

Most SaaS founders fall into the "E-Myth" trap:
- They're great technical operators but build businesses that depend on them
- They hire too early, spend too much, and create a company that breaks when they leave
- They chase growth hacks before achieving product-market fit

Our approach (synthesized from The E-Myth Revisited + 0-to-10M SaaS playbook):
1. **Sell before you build** — validate with conversations and pre-sales, not code
2. **Stay small** — <10 employees at every stage until you hit 10M ARR
3. **Bootstrap** — customers fund growth, not investors
4. **Build the franchise prototype** — systems that run without you
5. **Exit intentionally** — the target is an 8-figure sale, not a forever company

## What "Done" Looks Like

**Phase 1: Validation (0 to 100K ARR)**
- [ ] We've sold to at least 10 customers before building
- [ ] Product-market fit confirmed (organic growth, high retention, customers would be livid if product disappeared)
- [ ] Business runs without us doing manual fulfillment

**Phase 2: Systematization (100K to 1M ARR)**
- [ ] Every process documented in SOPs
- [ ] We've achieved ramen profitability (we can survive on personal expenses)
- [ ] Product works without us touching it
- [ ] Team of 2-5 people, not more

**Phase 3: Scale & Exit (1M to 10M ARR → Exit)**
- [ ] 2-3 proven acquisition channels at 1M+ ARR
- [ ] Business runs with <10 employees
- [ ] Clean financials, documented processes, no key-person dependency
- [ ] Target buyer identified (strategic acquirer or PE firm)
- [ ] Exit achieved at 8-figure valuation

## Core Principles (Non-Negotiables)

1. **No hiring before 1M ARR** — co-founders only
2. **No VC** — let customers fund growth
3. **No "same but cheaper"** — be different in a way that matters
4. **No automation before PMF** — do things that don't scale first
5. **No spending on non-essentials** — CRM (use spreadsheets), no office, no content studio
6. **Work ON the business, not IN it** — every decision: does this make us more or less replaceable?
7. **The goal is the exit** — build something someone will buy
